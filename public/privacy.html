Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

AgentOS is an assistant that carries out everyday tasks in your own apps (Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive and Google Forms) after you approve them. This page explains what data AgentOS uses and how. It is a student hackathon project run by Anjankumar LN and team.

Demo Mode

Demo Mode needs no account. It runs entirely in your browser as a simulation; nothing you type is sent to our servers.

What we store when you use Live Mode

  • Your account: name (optional), email address, and a salted hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Your missions: the goals you type, the plan, approvals you give, and the results (for example the link to an event AgentOS created), so you can see progress and proof.
  • Your preferences: timezone, working hours, signature and contact groups you choose to save.
  • Google connection: if you connect Google, the access tokens Google issues, encrypted at rest. They are never shown in the app, never sent to your browser and never written to logs.

What AgentOS does with your Google account

Only what you ask for in a mission, and every action that affects other people waits for your approval:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): read free/busy times and create or update the events you approve.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): send the emails you approve, exactly as shown to you. AgentOS cannot read your mailbox.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): create documents for your missions. AgentOS can only see files it created.
  • Google Forms (forms.body): create forms (for example a registration form) for your missions.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email): to show which Google account is connected.

AgentOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train AI models.

AI planning

To turn your goal into a plan, the text of your goal (and your saved preferences such as timezone and contact group names) is sent to an AI model provider (Google Gemini). Your Google tokens and your Google data are never sent to the AI provider.

Where data is kept

The service runs on Vercel (website), Render (application server) and Neon (database). Data is used only to run AgentOS for you.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google at any time in Apps; AgentOS then revokes its access at Google and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data: email anjankumarln120@gmail.com and we will delete your account and missions.
